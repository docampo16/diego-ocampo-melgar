CONTEXT

From potential distribution to risk

Pudú face habitat loss and fragmentation, predation by domestic dogs, competition with exotic species, poaching and vehicle mortality. To examine the road component specifically, the work adapted a traditional habitat model into a spatially explicit risk analysis.

ANALYSIS

Exposure as the link between species and threat

The analysis treated habitat exposure to roads as a critical factor in explaining potential mortality. Combining suitability, the road network and sighting or mortality records helped identify locations where animals are more likely to interact with vehicles.

ANALYSIS

Prioritising where to act

Results were used to identify critical areas and guide preventive measures and mitigation strategies in Chiloé Province. The approach illustrates how an SDM can be extended from an ecological distribution question to a management question about threat, exposure and risk.

PROJECT VALUE

What this project demonstrates

Ability to extend species-distribution modelling into threat and exposure questions, turning ecological results into spatial conservation priorities.

The model shifts the question from “where could the species occur?” to “where does species distribution overlap with a threat capable of causing mortality?”.
